# VD V1 virtual time contract

## Clock and feed

`VirtualClock(initial_T_utc, cursor, schedule_version)` starts at the first admitted event's availability time, or an explicit earlier frozen UTC start bound to the campaign manifest. `T` is monotone and cannot move backward. `VirtualMarketFeed` exposes immutable records with source timestamp, interval `[open, close)`, availability timestamp, timeframe, sequence number, source hash, and quality classification. It emits only records with `availability_at <= T`; missing or inconsistent availability fails closed. All timestamps are timezone-aware UTC. Session calendars use the signed SSC/TD-8E conventions, never host-local time.

At each step, advance T to the next event availability time, then apply this total order: (1) finalize source bars whose close and publication are admissible; (2) update admitted TD-8E/MI context; (3) call canonical SSC decision once for the decision-cycle key; (4) validate and submit any resulting proposal; (5) process orders eligible at this T using only later admissible execution observations; (6) mark/account positions and append snapshots. Within one priority, sort by `(availability_at, source_id, sequence, deterministic_id)`. An order submitted from a bar close cannot fill from that bar's open/high/low/close. A fill requires an observation whose interval starts strictly after the decision's information cutoff, unless a separately validated tick observation has event time strictly later and its availability is proven.

H1/M15/M1 bars become knowable only after their respective close plus publication lag. Incomplete bars are unavailable to SSC unless its frozen contract explicitly permits them. Derived higher-timeframe bars inherit the latest availability of their constituent inputs. Late revisions are separate versioned events and cannot rewrite prior decisions. If a required timeframe close, provenance, or ordering is missing, emit an explicit `TEMPORAL_REJECTED` decision-cycle status.

End of data stops new submissions, resolves only events with available observations, and leaves open orders/positions `UNRESOLVED_END_OF_DATA` with a final account snapshot; it never invents a terminal fill or price. A policy that expires/cancels orders at end of data must be frozen in advance. Restart must validate all hashes and replay from checkpoint to an identical ledger root. Playback at 1×, accelerated, and unthrottled speed must yield byte-identical semantic records after excluding wall-clock telemetry.
