## Findings

- **EURUSD Asian→London L3 FAIL:** the only revised case is `recorded:EURUSD:ASIAN_LONDON:2026-07-31`.
  A RANGE emission at prefix 7 of 16 is later revised: 5 prefix mismatches and 25 of 25
  future-mutation mismatches. No SWEEP or TREND first emission was revised (71 SWEEP and 18 TREND
  cases). This is an L3 evidence failure on the RANGE branch, recorded as-is. The frozen logic is not changed.
- **GBPUSD:** L1–L4 and L6 PASS in both sessions on 11 conforming entries. The harness's own verdict
  string reads LOGIC_VERIFIED because it treats L5 WARN as non-blocking. **That verdict is not
  adopted:** L5 is INSUFFICIENT (spread not consumed, commission unknown). No registry change is
  proposed.
- **USDJPY and XAUUSD:** 0 conforming entries. Pip size is not evidenced (L5 BLOCK). The unchanged
  harness also applies the fixed 0.2-pip `TEST_SPREAD` at digits 5, which `R.max_spread` rejects.
  This matches #129's earlier result.
- **Spread:** the 60-day files carry `spread_points`, but the unchanged harness does not consume it.
  Cost-in-R therefore remains unmeasured.
