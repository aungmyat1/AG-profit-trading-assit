# ES-S6 Summary

**Primary question:** is available source evidence sufficient to reconstruct the presenter's actual deterministic trade-eligibility contract, or should exact reconstruction stop in favor of a source-inspired research fork of the deterministic core?

**Answer:** the Detection and Management contracts (what counts as a Sweep; what happens after entry) are fully source-supported and correctly implemented. The Eligibility contract (which of the 66 mechanically-valid sweeps becomes one of the presenter's 18 actual trades) is not reconstructable from available material — the presenter's own narrative confirms a discretionary selection process exists, but supplies no quantitative rule for it, and every numeric candidate found in the source chat log was constructed by chasing benchmark outcomes rather than derived from the presenter's own statements.

**Frozen 66-occurrence population (descriptive only):** 15 of 19 calendar days produced an Asian range; of those, 1 day (Oct 5) produced zero structural events at all; occurrence counts per day range from 0 to 8 (max), with 11 of 15 days producing 4 or more occurrences and 46 same-direction repeat occurrences across the period. This descriptive shape is consistent with — but does not by itself prove — a missing multiplicity-control rule.

**Decision: `PATH_B_RESEARCH_FORK`.** `ST_SESSION_TRADING_SOURCE_V1 v0.1.0` remains frozen exactly as `FROZEN_LOW_CORRESPONDENCE_INTERPRETATION`. No v0.2 is justified from currently available evidence. A future, separately-authorized mission may consider forking the deterministic Detection+Management core into an independent research lineage that does not claim source-eligibility fidelity — not performed here.
